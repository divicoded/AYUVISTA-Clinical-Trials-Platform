## 3. Top 10 Judge Q&A Defense Master Cheat Sheet

### Q1: "Is this actual live patient data from AIIA hospital?"
* **Your Answer**: *"No, sir/ma'am. In strict compliance with India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023 and ethical research guidelines, our system utilizes a high-fidelity synthetic patient population tokenized as `SYN-P00001` through `SYN-P01520`. It mirrors the exact clinical and statistical reality of multicenter Ayurvedic trials without exposing sensitive real-world Protected Health Information (PHI)."*

### Q2: "Why does an Ayurvedic institute need CDISC standards?"
* **Your Answer**: *"Historically, Ayurvedic trials have been criticized internationally for lack of standardized reporting. Global drug regulatory agencies like the US FDA and the European Medicines Agency (EMA) mandate CDISC SDTM and ADaM data packages for new drug applications. By generating compliant CDISC datasets and Define-XML natively from AIIA trials, AYUVISTA enables Ayurvedic medicines to be submitted for global clinical registration and patent protection."*

### Q3: "How does the 24-hour safety clock work technically?"
* **Your Answer**: *"Under NDCT Rules 2019, the reporting window begins the instant an adverse event is flagged as Serious. Our backend records the initial report timestamp in UTC. The frontend computes the countdown dynamically against Indian Standard Time (IST). If the deadline drops below 6 hours, the system elevates the case to urgent priority, triggers visual alert pulses, and alerts the designated Pharmacovigilance Officer."*

### Q4: "What prevents someone from tampering with clinical records?"
* **Your Answer**: *"We have implemented an append-only ALCOA+ audit trail interceptor at the SQLAlchemy ORM layer. Every time a record is created, updated, or transitioned, the system automatically writes a new immutable audit record capturing the user ID, role, exact timestamp, action name, and a complete JSON before-and-after diff. No `UPDATE` or `DELETE` operations are permitted on the audit ledger table."*

### Q5: "How does the system handle multi-herb formulations common in Ayurveda?"
* **Your Answer**: *"Unlike Western allopathic trials that track single chemical entities, AYUVISTA data models support complex classical Ayurvedic formulations (like Guduchi-Pippali Rasayana or Ashwagandha Ghrita). The system tracks multi-ingredient batch numbers, classical preparation methods, dosage forms (Vati, Kwatha, Churna), and maps adverse events using synthetic MedDRA terminology."*

### Q6: "Can a user bypass the MedDRA coding step in Safety?"
* **Your Answer**: *"No. We have built strict programmatic workflow gates. At Step 4 (CODING), the 'Advance to Step 5' button is disabled until a valid Preferred Term (PT) and System Organ Class (SOC) are selected and saved from the MedDRA dictionary lookup adapter. This guarantees that un-coded safety reports cannot reach regulatory assessment."*

### Q7: "How is Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) enforced?"
* **Your Answer**: *"RBAC is enforced on both client and server sides. In the FastAPI backend, custom dependency middleware inspects the JWT claims on every request. For example, a Study Coordinator cannot approve a protocol or trigger a CTRI sync; only an Ethics Committee or Leadership role can perform those actions. On the frontend, unauthorized navigation routes are automatically protected."*

### Q8: "How does AYUVISTA connect with Ayushman Bharat (ABDM)?"
* **Your Answer**: *"AYUVISTA includes an ABDM interoperability sandbox that simulates the National Health Authority's M1, M2, and M3 milestones. It demonstrates ABHA ID validation and digital participant informed consent exchange via FHIR R4 standard JSON payloads."*

### Q9: "What was the hardest technical challenge you solved?"
* **Your Answer**: *"The hardest challenge was bridging clinical rigor with user experience. Standard clinical trial software like Veeva or Medidata is notoriously congested, slow, and complex. We engineered a Material Design 3 Expressive UI with spacious tabbed slide-out drawers, animated progress steppers, and live S-curve graphs, while simultaneously maintaining strict compliance with CDISC SDTM schemas, ALCOA+ before/after diffs, and 24h statutory clocks."*

### Q10: "What is your deployment and hosting cost?"
* **Your Answer**: *"AYUVISTA is designed to be extremely cost-effective. Because it is built with open-source technologies (FastAPI, SQLite/PostgreSQL, React, Tailwind), there are zero recurring license fees like SAS or Oracle Clinical.
